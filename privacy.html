Privacy

We keep things simple. VixoGames does not run its own user accounts or payment system.

Local storage

We use your browser’s localStorage to remember favorites and recently played games. You can clear this anytime via your browser settings (site data / cookies).

Third-party games (GamePix)

When you play a game, it loads from play.gamepix.com inside an iframe. GamePix may use its own cookies or storage according to their policies. We do not control that embedded experience.

Analytics & ads

We do not currently inject third-party analytics or ad trackers on the VixoGames site. If that changes, this page will be updated.

Offline cache

A small service worker may cache static files (HTML, CSS, JS) so the site shell can load faster or work offline for navigation. Game feeds and players still need an internet connection.

Children

Some games are aimed at kids; parents should supervise play. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children on this site.
