About VixoGames

VixoGames is a free hub for browser games — pick a title, press play, and start instantly. No downloads, no accounts, and no payment walls.

How it works

Games are streamed from GamePix, a partner platform that hosts thousands of HTML5 titles. We organize them into categories, search, favorites, and recommendations so you can find something fun in seconds.

What we store on your device

Your favorites and recently played list are saved locally in your browser (localStorage). They never leave your device unless you clear site data.

Contact & feedback

Use our contact form to report bugs, suggest games, or reach out about partnerships.
